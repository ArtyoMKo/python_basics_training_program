# Marking guide — the diagnostic

**Sat before day 1. 40 points. Six questions.**

This is the only measurement of what the grade-10 course actually produced, taken before
this course can affect it. Mark it the day it is sat; the results change how day 1 runs.

| Q | Measures | Points | Grade-10 day it came from |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `for` over a collection + `if` | 8 | 11–13 |
| 2 | a counter, and an average | 7 | 12 |
| 3 | `def` and **`return`** | 8 | 17–18 |
| 4 | building a dictionary in a loop | 7 | 8, 14–15 |
| 5 | reading an error message | 5 | every day |
| 6 | reading a file | 5 | 22 |

## What a wrong answer tells you

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Q1 has no loop** — one block per student | The single most important outcome of the grade-10 course did not land | **Do not start day 2 as if it had.** Extend day 1's part C and run a loop clinic before day 2 |
| **Q3 uses `print` instead of `return`** | Functions are understood as "a way to print", not as something that hands a value back | Days 2 and 3 slower, with more examples. This course leans on `return` from day 2 |
| **Q4 left blank, Q1 fine** | Dictionaries are shakier than loops | Day 5 and day 6 are where this hurts. Add five minutes of revision at the start of each |
| **Q5 blank** | Error messages were never practised | Spend two minutes a day reading a traceback aloud, from day 2 onward |
| **Q6 blank, everything else fine** | Files were the last thing taught and did not stick | Harmless until day 16. Mention it then rather than now |

## Reading the group, not the person

| Group average | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **above 30** | A strong starting point | Days 2–5 can move faster than planned |
| **20–30** | The expected range | Run as designed |
| **below 15** | **Serious signal** | Raise it with colleagues before day 2. The honest option on the table is repeating the grade-10 course rather than starting this one |

## Say this out loud before the sitting

> «Այս թեստը գնահատում է **ծրագիրը**, ոչ թե ձեզ։ Արդյունքը ոչ մի տեղ չի գնում և ոչ մի
> բանի վրա չի ազդում, բացի նրանից, թե ես ինչպես եմ դասավանդելու հաջորդ շաբաթները։»

Participants who believe they are being judged perform differently, and the measurement
is worthless if they do.
