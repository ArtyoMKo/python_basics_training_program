# Marking guide — the midpoint

**Sat after day 11. 50 points. Six questions, covering days 2–11.**

The block boundary is deliberate: everything this test asks about has been taught, and
nothing after it is a library. It is the decision point for whether the libraries block
can start.

| Q | Measures | Days | Points |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | default arguments, calling by name | 2 | 8 |
| 2 | `return a, b` and unpacking | 3 | 7 |
| 3 | list comprehension | 4–5 | 8 |
| 4 | **`class`, `__init__`, `self`, a method** | 6–8 | **12** |
| 5 | inheritance and `super()` | 9 | 10 |
| 6 | polymorphism — a loop with no `if` | 10 | 5 |

## Question 4 is the one that decides

At 12 of 50 it is the largest single question in any of the three sittings, because
whether a participant can write a working `__init__` decides whether day 12 can happen
at all. Everything from day 12 onward assumes objects.

**Record separately how many wrote a working `__init__`**, not just the score.

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **More than half score under 6 on Q4** | **Do not start day 12.** Add one revision session on days 6–8. Losing a session here is cheaper than losing the whole second half |
| `class` written, but no `__init__` | The template/instance distinction has not landed. Revisit "defining is not creating" |
| `__init__` written without `self` | Mechanical, and fixes quickly. Ten minutes at the start of day 12 |

## The rest

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Q5 without `super()`** — parent fields copied by hand | Inheritance is understood as copying, which is the thing it replaces | Ten minutes at the start of day 12. Show day 9's side-by-side table again |
| **Q3 answered with a loop** | Comprehensions did not stick. The answer is correct, so give half marks | Not urgent. Days 13 and 17 use them naturally; point at them when they appear |
| **Q6 answered with `isinstance`** | Polymorphism was understood as "check the type". Half marks | Mention once on day 10's material; it does not block anything later |
| **Q1 and Q2 strong, Q4–Q6 weak** | Functions landed, objects did not | This is the commonest shape. The revision session above is the answer |

## Reading the group

| Group average | What to do |
|---|---|
| **above 40** | The block landed. Continue at the planned pace |
| **25–40** | Expected. Ten minutes of revision at the start of day 12 |
| **below 25** | Add a full revision session. Record it — it is a finding about the programme, not about the group |
